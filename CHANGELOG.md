## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-26

### Added
- Highlight arbitrary-length text, not just single words.
- Toggle highlighting for the word under the cursor with `F8`.
- Highlight multiple words at once, each with a distinct color from a configurable palette.
- Persist highlights per workspace and restore them when the project is reopened.
- Configurable case sensitivity, contrast text color, and default text color.
